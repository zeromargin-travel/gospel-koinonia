Concept 3-C Deep Exploration

"3-C" Vine, Dove & Cross Variations

Four distinct artistic and geometric variations centered on 3-C (The Vine & Dove GK Cross), maintaining the tranquil Sage Green palette with warm amber heart.

3-C1 (Refined Master)

3-C1. Sculpted Wings & Vine

Pure Master of Concept 3-C

The direct refinement of 3-C. The right side features solid, sculpted dove wings (representing the Holy Spirit and praise) forming the K, while the left side forms an embracing living vine G, rooted on the central Cross with an amber flame at the heart.

Key Highlights:
  • Sculpted Wings: Solid white feathers for high contrast & impact
  • Living Vine (G): Tender branch of fellowship (John 15:5)
  • Amber Flame: Warm light of the Holy Spirit at the cross center

3-C2 (Linear Harmony)

3-C2. Linear Wing Harmonies

Airy, Elegant & Balanced Stroke Weight

Instead of solid fills, the dove wings of K are rendered with elegant layered line strokes. This creates a visually balanced weight with the vine stroke of G, resulting in an airy, sophisticated, and modern look.

Key Highlights:
  • Matched Stroke Weight: Uniform line harmony across G, K, and Cross
  • Dual Feathers: Layered soft mint accents suggesting plumage
  • Gentle Grace: Clean, breezy, and welcoming atmosphere

3-C3 (Olive & Dove)

3-C3. The Olive Branch & Dove

Rich Biblical Narrative of Peace

Directly evokes Noah's dove returning with an olive leaf (Genesis 8:11) and Christ's baptism (Matthew 3:16). The vine of G features a soft budding leaf of new life, while K forms the joyful wings of the dove of peace.

Key Highlights:
  • Olive Leaf Accent: Promise of peace, fresh starts & healing
  • Ascending Dove: The Holy Spirit's movement in Enschede
  • High Storytelling: Rich biblical message in a modern seal

3-C4 (Radiant Cross Focus)

3-C4. The Radiant Cross

Strong Crossbar + Wing Integration

Strengthens the Latin Cross presence by including a visible horizontal crossbar, intersecting the vertical beam. The dove wings of K and the vine of G anchor directly onto this cross, giving it an unmistakable Christian cross identity from any distance.

Key Highlights:
  • Distinct Crossbar: Traditional Latin Cross shape clearly emphasized
  • G & K Anchored: Letters grow naturally from the cross itself
  • Glowing Center: Dual-tone amber & white spark of grace
