OUR HEART & ESSENCE

What is Gospel Koinonia?

The Greek word koinonia speaks of profound communion, shared life, and intimate partnership. Here is why we choose fellowship over religious formality.

Fellowship, Not Institution

We are not an ecclesiastical hierarchy or formal denomination. We are a living, breathing circle of believers and curious seekers walking side by side as equal brothers and sisters.

The Simple Gospel (Evangel)

Rooted in the unconditional love, redemption, and grace of Jesus Christ. No complicated religious barriers — just good news that brings freedom, healing, and peace to real human hearts.

An Open Table for Everyone

Whether you are an international student at UTwente, a local Enschede resident, a lifelong Christian, or someone with honest doubts and questions — there is always an open chair for you.

Our Commitment

A Safe Haven in an Anxious World

In a fast-paced society where isolation is widespread, we cultivate an authentic space of unhurried presence. Here you can be your true self, speak openly, sing wholeheartedly, and be prayed for without judgment.

HOW WE SHARE LIFE

Core Activities of Our Fellowship

Every gathering is designed around warmth, spiritual nourishment, and rich human connection.

Worship Bi-weekly

Gospel Praise & Worship

Vibrant, heartfelt acoustic gospel songs and hymns that stir the spirit. We sing not for performance, but to give voice to gratitude, reverence, and honest longing.

  • Acoustic & Gospel rhythms
  • Multi-language song selections
  • Open to music enthusiasts & players
Table Every Gathering

Agape Table Fellowship

Inspired by early Christian love feasts, we share warm, homemade meals and tea together. Conversations flow freely around delicious comfort food and fresh bakery.

  • Potluck & home-cooked specialties
  • Dutch, Asian & International flavors
  • Vegetarian-friendly options
Word Weekly

Gospel Reflection & Life Dialogue

Interactive Scripture discussions centered on practical life application, questions of purpose, relationships, and finding peace in daily challenges.

  • No monologue lectures — open Q&A
  • Respectful of all perspectives & doubts
  • English-language discussions
Care Ongoing

Prayer & Mutual Support

Life has trials, transitions, and loneliness. We hold one another in prayer, offer practical encouragement, and stand with each other during tough seasons.

  • Confidential prayer requests
  • Student & newcomer relocation help
  • Heartfelt pastoral friendship

Our gatherings are conducted predominantly in English to welcome people from diverse international and local backgrounds. Many of us also speak Dutch, Japanese, and other languages, so translations and informal help are always available.
