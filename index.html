OUR HEART & ESSENCE

What is Gospel Koinonia?

The Greek word koinonia speaks of profound communion, shared life, and genuine Christian fellowship in the grace of Jesus Christ.

Authentic Fellowship

Rooted in the New Testament vision of koinonia, we cultivate genuine brotherly love, mutual prayer, and heartfelt care where each person is cherished as family in Christ.

The Simple Gospel (Evangel)

Rooted in the unconditional love, redemption, and grace of Jesus Christ. No complicated religious barriers — just good news that brings freedom, healing, and peace to real human hearts.

A Warm Seat for Everyone

Whether you are an international student at UTwente, a local Enschede resident, a lifelong believer, or someone exploring faith — there is always a warm cup of tea and a friendly seat for you.

Our Commitment

A Safe Haven in an Anxious World

In a fast-paced society where isolation is widespread, we cultivate an authentic space of unhurried presence. Here you can be your true self, speak openly, sing wholeheartedly, and be prayed for without judgment.

OUR THEOLOGICAL FOUNDATIONS

What We Believe

Gospel Koinonia stands squarely within historic, orthodox Protestant and evangelical Christian faith. We confess Jesus Christ as Lord, uphold the Holy Scriptures as God’s inspired Word, and affirm the historic confession of the universal Church.

The Apostles' Creed

Historic Confession of the Christian Church

I believe in God, the Father almighty,
creator of heaven and earth.

I believe in Jesus Christ, his only Son, our Lord,
who was conceived by the Holy Spirit
and born of the virgin Mary.
He suffered under Pontius Pilate,
was crucified, died, and was buried;
he descended to hell.
The third day he rose again from the dead.
He ascended to heaven
and is seated at the right hand of God the Father almighty.
From there he will come to judge the living and the dead.

I believe in the Holy Spirit,
the holy universal Church,
the communion of saints,
the forgiveness of sins,
the resurrection of the body,
and the life everlasting. Amen.

The Authority of Scripture

We believe the Bible (Old and New Testaments) is the inspired, trustworthy Word of God, fully reliable and our supreme authority in all matters of faith, doctrine, and daily life.

Salvation by Grace Alone

Salvation is the unmerited gift of God's grace, received solely through personal faith in Jesus Christ, whose atoning cross and bodily resurrection deliver us from sin and death.

The Holy Spirit & Renewal

The Holy Spirit indwells every believer, regenerating hearts, offering comfort, imparting spiritual gifts for service, and producing the fruit of love, peace, and holiness.

Unity in the Body of Christ

We are not a closed or exclusive sect. We joyfully affirm our fellowship with all faithful Christian churches and believers across traditions who confess Jesus Christ as Lord.
